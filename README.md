# SPLENDID (SPLD)

**Excellence Beyond Expectations.**

SPLENDID is an early-stage Solana-based community and digital utility project. Its goal is to build useful software and community experiences with transparent development, responsible token design, and clear public documentation.

> **Current status: Development planning and Devnet prototype preparation.**
>
> SPLENDID is not currently presenting SPLD as a live investment, and no mainnet token, public sale, liquidity pool, exchange listing, yield programme, or token-holder profit entitlement is being announced by this repository.

## What we are building

The project is being developed in stages:

1. **Public project information** — clear explanations, roadmap, decisions, risks, and progress.
2. **Solana Devnet token prototype** — test-only mint, metadata, and wallet-to-wallet transfer testing.
3. **SPLENDID Hub** — a web interface for project information, wallet connection, token display, and community participation.
4. **Community tools** — initially off-chain polls, feedback, learning activities, and transparent participation records.
5. **Practical digital services** — software and digital products that can create revenue independently of token speculation.

Features are proposals until implemented, tested, and documented as complete.

## Why we are building

We believe a credible digital project should demonstrate its work rather than rely on slogans. SPLENDID aims to combine blockchain technology, useful digital tools, community participation, and open development.

We will publish decisions and changes, distinguish working features from proposals, and avoid claims that cannot be verified.

## Revenue approach

The initial business direction is to explore revenue from genuine products and services, such as software development, dashboards, automation tools, educational materials, and optional premium digital services.

No revenue is guaranteed. SPLD does not currently promise dividends, profit sharing, interest, yield, price appreciation, or redemption for cash. Any future token utility or commercial offering will require separate product, legal, and security review.

## Development principles

- Build and test on Devnet before considering Mainnet.
- Never request or publish anyone's seed phrase or private key.
- Keep wallet secrets out of source control and frontend code.
- Verify mint addresses, authorities, metadata, and transactions.
- Publish material token design changes.
- Do not fabricate users, partnerships, audits, exchange listings, or milestones.
- Do not market test tokens as investments.
- Seek qualified legal advice before any public token issuance or sale.
